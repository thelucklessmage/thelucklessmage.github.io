About Me

The story

The Luckless Mage was born from a passion for tabletop RPGs and handcrafted art. Every dice set is created with patience, precision, and a touch of chaos.

I started making resin dice as a way to bring unique, magical objects into the hands of players who value storytelling and immersion.

Craftsmanship

Each piece is individually cast, sanded, polished and inspected. No two dice are ever exactly the same — and that is part of their magic.

Based in Switzerland, I focus on quality, detail, and unique fantasy aesthetics.
